# Databel Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer churn for Databel Telecom. The dashboard provides actionable insights into customer behavior, demographics, and service interactions to help the company identify the root causes of churn and improve retention strategies.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Customers:** 6,687
- **Churned Customers:** 1,796
- **Overall Churn Rate:** 26.86%
- **Total Charge:** $7.25M
- **Average Age:** 47.45 years

## 📈 Visualizations & Analysis
- **Top 10 Churn Reasons (Horizontal Bar Chart):** Highlights the primary reasons for customer departure.
- **Churn Rate by State (Map):** Geographical distribution identifying high-churn hotspots.
- **Churned by Churn Category:** Categorizes specific reasons into main categories (e.g., Competitor, Attitude, Price).
- **Churn Rate by Customer Service Calls (Line Chart):** Illustrates how repeated, unresolved calls to customer service drastically increase the probability of churn.
- **Churn Rate Status (Gauge Chart):** Visualizes the overall churn percentage against company targets.
- **Total Customers & Churn Rate by Age (Combo Chart):** Age is grouped into 5-year bins to track generational churn trends, revealing higher churn among older demographics (60+).
- **Average Monthly Charge & Churn Rate by Group (Combo Chart):** Correlates billing amounts and group sizes with retention.
- **Churn Rate by Contract Category & Gender (Clustered Column Chart):** Compares the high churn rate of month-to-month contracts against yearly commitments.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filters for State, Gender, and Group to enable deep-dive segment analysis.

## 💡 Key Insights
1. **Customer Service Frustration:** Churn rates spike exponentially after customers make multiple calls to support without resolution.
2. **Demographic Shifts:** Customers over the age of 60 demonstrate a significantly higher churn rate.
3. **Contract Vulnerability:** Month-to-month contracts are the most at-risk segment, showing a much higher churn probability than annual contracts.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI:** Data cleaning, DAX measures, data modeling, and interactive data visualization.
